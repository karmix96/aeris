# S8 audit, 2026-09-10 — can the results be trusted?

The question asked: *audit every study, its conditions, assumptions, ground truth and
verification; can we trust what we have?* This document answers it study by study. It
supersedes nothing in `AUDIT_2026-09-05.md`, which covers the mesher; this covers the CFD
campaign built on it from 6 to 10 September.

It re-checked rather than restated, and the re-checking found a defect that had corrupted
results and a published conclusion. That is recorded first.

---

## Verdict

| class of result | trust | why |
|---|---|---|

| Convergence of every accepted run | **Trusted** | gate with two routes, frozen-solver detection verified still firing; stopping rule validated on four geometries; **every equation now tested separately, Fluent-style — all 77 runs clear it (B7)** |

**In one sentence:** the numerical machinery is sound and verified; the aerodynamic *accuracy*
of drag is not yet established against anything but further refinement of the same model.

## Defect 23 — found by this audit

**23a.** `solve_s8.py` defaults `--area-ref` to 0.394918 m², index 83's half-area (inherited
as "the C03 contract"). The pilot never passed anything else, so **all ten geometries were
normalised by index 83's area.** Their own areas run from 0.2978 to 0.5162 m² — −24.6 % to
+30.7 %. Forces were correct; every coefficient of nine geometries was scaled by
`solver_area / own_area`.

**23b.** `verify_against_avl.py`'s docstring states that it *"refuses to compare if they
disagree."* There was no code that did. The check was documented and never written.

**What it corrupted.** The claim, made in conversation, in `STATE_2026-09-10.md` and on the
published status page, that the CFD–AVL correction is geometry-specific (spread across
geometries 3.5× the spread across incidence; −25 % to +32 % at α = 8°). Every large
"disagreement" matched its geometry's area ratio to within a percent:

| geometry | own area vs 83 | "CFD−AVL" as reported | corrected |
|---|---|---|---|
| g13 | +30.5 % | +31.6 % | **+0.7 %** |
| g47 | −24.6 % | −25.0 % | **−0.5 %** |
| g81 | +17.6 % | +17.5 % | **−0.1 %** |
| g36 | +7.5 % | +6.8 % | **−0.6 %** |

Spread of CFD−AVL CL across geometries: **0.0330 → 0.0030**. The correction is not
geometry-specific; CFD and AVL agree to about ±2 % on every wing. The multifidelity strategy
that was called into question looks *viable*.

**Why it was invisible.** Index 83's own area is 0.394920 m² against the contract's
0.394918 — the one geometry every check ran on was the one the constant happened to fit.

**What it did not touch.** Anything computed within one geometry uses one area throughout, so
ratios and differences are exact: grid refinement, the directional study, the chordwise GCI,
the stopping-rule checks, gate verdicts, y+, mass balance and surface cp. The neutral point is
area-independent by construction — it moved by 5.6e-17 m under the correction.

**Fixed.**
- `reference_areas.json`: each geometry's half-area, from the loft (AVL `s_ref / 2`), so it is
  identical at every grid level. A per-mesh area would differ between levels and plant a fake
  grid-independent offset inside the GCI. The mesh-derived areas agree to within 0.21 %.
- `run_campaign.py` passes each geometry's own area to every solve, and **refuses** a geometry
  with no recorded area rather than falling back to index 83's.
- `verify_against_avl.py` now implements the refusal its docstring promised (0.5 % tolerance).
- `dataset_row.py` rescales mis-normalised rows, keeps the original and the factor, and refuses
  to pair CFD with AVL when their areas differ. The archive was rebuilt: 44 rows rescaled.
- Evidence: `reports/s8_reference_area_audit.json`.

## Study by study

Each: the question, the conditions, what it assumed, what it was measured against, how it was
verified, and whether to trust it.

### Mesh and infrastructure

**A1 · Mesh validity and repeatability.** *Conditions:* every build, `gci_C` to `gci_C_chord2`,
ten geometries. *Reference:* the exact loft; a second independent build. *Verified:* 0 folded
cells on every build; wall-layer error 2.2e-16 m (one at 1.2e-16); `compare_meshes` 37/37
identical across two builds of index 83. **Trusted.**

**A2 · Memory laws.** *Conditions:* ANK→NK law 1.51 + 9.46 GiB/Mcell, two measurements; ANK-only
law 2.69 + 7.23 GiB/Mcell, two points — one of them resident memory sampled during the ANK phase
of an NK-configured run. *Verified against:* `gci_M` NK peak 12.73 GiB against 12.02 predicted
(+6 %). **Usable for sizing, with margin.** Two-point fits; the ANK law's lower point is
indirect. The cloud batch sizes `gci_FF` on an *estimated* 4.7M cells.

**A3 · Pilot geometry selection.** *Method:* maximin over the 20 normalised design variables.
*Verified:* reproduces the ten indices and all four coverage figures in the plan exactly.
**Trusted** — but see D2: maximin in design-variable space did not produce size variation.

### Solver configuration and convergence

**B1 · Regression after the frame change (PLAN §3.2).** *Conditions:* `gci_C`, α 0, index 83.
*Reference:* the pre-fix `oh_L3` run, on a mesh since destroyed — a regression reference only.
*Result:* CL moved 2.75e-4, CD 1.5e-5. **Trusted as a regression check.**

**B2 · The 1e-6 stopping rule.** *Conditions:* four gci_C runs of index 83 replayed; then g12
α 0, g65 α 0 and g29 α 4 run 20–40× past the target. *Reference:* the same run taken further.
*Verified:* worst force movement beyond 1e-6 is 0.073 % (CDp, g65), against a 25–34 %
discretization signal. **Trusted.** Two notes: 1e-8 is not reliably reachable with ANK only
(plateau 2–5e-8), and CL's *relative* movement is inflated on designs that trim near the
sampled incidence (g65: CL −0.0087).

**B3 · The convergence gate.** Changed three times this campaign, each time against a
measurement: an adaptive force window (a fixed 100 iterations was 65 % of a 155-iteration run);
dead-linear-solve detection (a drifting residual hid a frozen Newton step); and two acceptance
routes instead of one hurdle (the tail-spread metric overstated error sevenfold, measured on g12).
*Verified:* all three known-frozen runs are still caught after every change. **Trusted.** The
risk it carries is structural: each change loosened acceptance, and each was justified — the
safeguard is that the frozen cases were re-tested every time.

**B4 · ANK-only in place of ANK→NK.** *Why:* at 1.11M cells NK froze (step 0.01, linear
residual 1.000, forces fixed to 13 significant figures) with this project's lean preconditioner.
*Verified:* ANK-only re-runs of `gci_C` reproduced the NK results to five decimals, including the
two runs that had frozen — whose forces turned out to be correct. **Trusted.**

**B5 · Multigrid (next step 3).** Index 83 `gci_C` α 0, idle host, 6 ranks. ANK-only baseline
827 s. `MGCycle 2w` with DADI to 1e-2 then ANK: 1,476 s, same answer. `3w`: ADflow stops in
`checkCoarse1to1` — "Non-matching block-to-block face on zone o_wing ... Support not
implemented yet": the O-H topology cannot be coarsened to a third level. **Rejected.** Side
result: the two converged runs differ by 0.32 % in CDp and 0.11 % in CL — the path noise of
the 1e-6 rule, about 10× below the grid effects measured and a floor on any comparison
between settings.

**B6 · Freestream turbulence (settings on trial).** Every S8 run used ADflow's default
`eddyVisInfRatio` 0.009, which ADflow converts to SA's ν̃ through `saNuKnownEddyRatio`: χ ≈ 1.3.
The TMR prescribes χ = 3–5. Index 83 `gci_C` at χ = 3 (ratio 0.21): CD +1.19 % (α 0) and
+1.00 % (α 4), CDv +2.15 % / +1.71 %, CDp +0.45 % / +0.35 %, CL unchanged. Above the path noise,
so real. **The earlier recommendation to keep the default for consistency is withdrawn**: the
default is not a value SA was validated with. Recommended: TMR χ = 3 for the cloud batch, with
index 83's `gci_C` and `gci_M` re-run at it so the reference GCI compares like with like.
Queued to explain it: SA without ft2 at the default χ; χ = 3 at `gci_M`.

**B7 · Per-equation convergence — Fluent's process (12 Sept).** ADflow stops on `totalRes`,
the norm over every equation at once, and the gate re-judged on the continuity residual alone.
Either can hide one lagging equation behind five healthy ones. Measured over 77 runs, each
residual normalised by the largest value in its own first five iterations (how Fluent scales
residuals; iteration zero is the freestream guess, where `nuturb` starts near 7e-4 against
continuity's 9e+2):

| equation | orders dropped: worst — median — best |
|---|---|
| continuity | 5.91 — 6.26 — 7.43 |
| momentum x / y / z | 4.88 — 5.11 — 6.28 / 4.55 — 4.89 — 5.64 / 4.99 — 5.19 — 6.04 |
| energy | 6.12 — 6.35 — 7.48 |
| turbulence (SA) | **3.32 — 3.89 — 5.33** |
| combined `totalRes` | 6.00 — 6.01 — 6.04 (the stopping rule) |

Turbulence is the laggard in **every** run. `convergence_gate.py` now applies Fluent's own
default limits per equation — 1e-3 for continuity, momentum and turbulence, 1e-6 for energy —
as a requirement on both acceptance routes. **All 77 runs clear them and no verdict changed.**
Tightening turbulence to 1e-4 would reject 48 of the 77, and the stopping-rule study says those
are converged: carried 20–40× further, no force moved by more than 0.073 %. So the limit sits
where Fluent sets it and force settling does the fine work. Every archived row now carries
`equation_orders` (schema v2); 69 of 70 rows have it — the exception is `l2check_g12_a0_1e8`,
whose log was lost in the 10 September session restart and which carries no gate verdict either.
`reports/s8_equation_convergence.json`. **Trusted.**

**B8 · Settings on trial, results (12–13 Sept).** Each a single change against an existing
run of index 83 at `gci_C` α 0, so the comparison is like for like.

| change | CD | CDv | reading |
|---|---|---|---|
| far field 40 → 60 root chords, **at α 8** | +0.23 % | — | the worry that this grows with lift is dead: +0.33 % at α 0, +0.23 % at α 8 |
| turbulence advection first → second order | −0.25 % | −0.69 % | negligible; the ADflow default stands, and it is CFL3D's default too |
| SA without the ft2 term, default χ | +0.16 % | +0.32 % | **the hypothesis was wrong**: ft2 is not the mechanism behind the freestream-turbulence effect |
| freestream χ 3 at `gci_M` | +1.15 % | +1.80 % | the same offset as at `gci_C` (+1.19 %, +2.15 %): **grid-independent**, so it can be carried as a correction |
| Menter SST | — | — | **aborts this ADflow build** (PETSc error, twice). First attempt wrote CL −1.17, CD 2.21 as converged; that hole is now closed |

**Defect 24 — a correct number under a wrong label.** `collect_dataset.py` read the geometry
from the run's *directory*, defaulting to 83 when it found none. `l2check_g12_a0_1e8`, solved on
g12's mesh from a directory with no geometry in its name, was archived as **geometry 83**. It
now reads the geometry from the grid path the solver was actually given — a fact, not a
convention — with the directory as fallback. Same principle the level already used.

**Defect 25 — an unjudged run counted as accepted.** The archive filter excluded runs the gate
*rejected*, but a MISSING verdict passed it. The same l2check run, whose log was lost in the
10 September restart so no gate could read it, sat in the archive unjudged. An unjudged run is
not training data. It is now excluded and listed in the manifest.

**G2 · Archive audit (13 Sept).** `audit_runs.py` applies every check this campaign learned to
make, to every archived row at once: gate verdict, physical force bounds and finiteness,
per-equation convergence, residual against the stopping rule, reference area is the geometry's
own, moment reference and chord, no folded cells, grid on the design surface, y+ below 1, not
converged in under ten iterations, freestream direction. **69/69 rows sound.**
`reports/s8_archive_audit.json`; it is now stage 4b of `cloud_run.sh`, so no batch can be
called finished without it.

### Grid refinement on index 83

**C1 · `gci_C` → `gci_M`.** *Conditions:* both ANK-only, 1e-6, four angles, all accepted.
*Reference:* none external — this measures discretization. *Result:* CL ≤ 0.58 %, CDv ≤ 1.44 %,
CMy ≤ 1.55 %; CD 10.7–16.5 % and CDp 15.3–34.5 % (relative to the fine value). **Trusted as a
trend.** Two levels give no observed order and no uncertainty band.

**C2 · Neutral point.** *Result:* 0.3557 → 0.3552 m, 0.05 % of c_ref, R² 0.9995 on four angles.
*Cross-check:* AVL's own neutral point 0.337 m, 3.7 % of MAC. Area-independent. **Trusted as a
location** — see the moment-reference assumption for why it is not a stability verdict.

**C3 · Far field (PLAN §2.2).** 40 → 60 root chords at `gci_C` α 0: CD +0.33 %, CDp +3.9e-5,
against −2.80e-3 from refinement. **Trusted.** Far-field treatment is not the drag error.

**C4 · Edge refinement.** Same cell count, leading- and trailing-edge spacing refined: 7.0 % /
6.7 % of the CDp gap. **Trusted** — and it refuted a hypothesis I had put forward.

**C5 · Directional refinement.** One axis at a time: chord 88.3 / 88.5 %, span 11.0 / 12.0 %,
wall-normal −15.4 / −15.4 %, edges 7.0 / 6.7 %; all eight runs accepted. **Trusted for index
83.** One geometry, two angles; not yet repeated on a wing of a different size.
*Update 15 Sept:* the wall-normal −15 % is a reading of CDp alone. On total drag, layers 65 → 84
at fixed s0 move CD +1.7 / +1.6 counts, s0 ÷ 1.3 alone −0.8 / −0.8, both together (as the family
refines them) +0.9 / +0.8 — against −28.4 / −28.1 for `gci_C → gci_M`. About 3 counts move from
viscous to pressure drag as the boundary layer is resolved better; the net is small and opposite
in sign to the chordwise error. `gci_C_normal_s0_2` (queue18) gives the direction a third level.

**C6 · Chordwise three-level GCI.** 44 / 57 / 74 chordwise intervals, `--ratios 1.2982 1.2955`
(the default cell-count ratio would have understated r by a cube root). CDp extrapolates to
0.00756 at α 0 (GCI 11.8 %) and 0.00629 at α 4 (13.4 %); CD to 0.01678 and 0.01588 (≈5.5 %).
**Indicative.** Observed order ≈ 3.25 exceeds the scheme's formal order, which usually means the
triplet is not fully asymptotic. And chord-only refinement may converge to a different limit
than refining everything, so it is not a substitute for the global fine levels.

**C7 · First-cell height alone.** s0 divided by 1.3, every count held; its s0 equals `gci_M`'s.
Recovers 0.8 % (α 0) and 1.1 % (α 4) of the CDp gap; CL moves 0.2 %, CDv −0.6 %. With C5, the
wall-normal direction — layers and first cell alike — is exonerated as the source of the
pressure-drag error. **Trusted.**

**C8 · Rank scaling.** `gci_C` α 0 at 1, 2, 4 and 6 ranks, fixed 400-cycle budget, on a host with
6 physical cores. Linear-solver throughput speeds up 1.75×, 2.57× and 2.96× (49 % efficiency at 6),
but outer iterations completed fall 45 → 41 → 28 → 24: ANK's per-rank preconditioner weakens as
the domain is split, so each nonlinear step needs more linear work. Time per nonlinear step is
flat beyond two ranks — 8.0, 5.0, 5.0, 5.1 s. **Incomplete as a test**: a fixed cycle budget
measures throughput rather than time to a converged answer, and 6 ranks saturate this host's
memory bandwidth. **It does not support** `cloud_batch.py`'s assumed 75 % efficiency on 24
ranks; the 24-hour estimate may be optimistic by up to 3×.

**C9 · `gci_FF`, built.** Index 83, no solve: **4,504,420 cells** against the 4.7M estimate,
0 folded, 1.6 GB peak to build. The cloud memory check and GCI inputs now use the measured
count. **Trusted.**

### Across geometries

**D1 · Ten-geometry pilot.** *Conditions:* `gci_C`, four angles, ANK-only, 1e-6. *Verified:*
10/10 meshes clean including the five former folders; 40/40 accepted. **Trusted for robustness.
Coefficients trusted only as corrected** (defect 23).

**D2 · The CDp offset across geometries.** *Result:* −0.00274 ± 0.00017 (6.1 %) over g29, g65
and g83 at four angles, after area correction. **Provisional.** The three span only
0.392–0.395 m² of a pilot range of 0.298–0.516 m². They differ in shape; they do not differ in
size, and size is the dimension most likely to change an absolute drag offset.

**D3 · y+ across planforms.** OML p99 0.68–0.83 on all ten; the tip cap's 264 cells at 2.9–4.5
by design (`tip_span_first_cell_in_s0 = 10`). Area-independent. **Trusted.**

**D4 · CFD versus AVL.** **The original conclusion is retracted** (defect 23). Corrected, CL
agrees to about ±2 % on every geometry. AVL is inviscid and carries no thickness, so a few per
cent is the expected gap. **Trusted as corrected.**

**D5 · Neutral point and CG limits per design (next step 12).** The project has one mass model,
`configs/mass/baseline_uav.yaml`, with every design's CG at 0.40 m, so no per-design CG exists
to judge stability against. Reported instead, per design: the neutral point (`gci_M` where it
exists, else `gci_C`) and the aft-most CG for 5 % and 10 % static margin. Neutral points lie
at 0.24–0.39 m, 22.7–29.4 % MAC — where a wing's aerodynamic centre belongs — and **all ten are
forward of 0.40 m**: with the CG there, every design would be unstable (static margin −1.4 %
to −35 % MAC). CFD puts the neutral point 16–26 mm aft of AVL on every design. MAC from the
AVL sections, exact for chord linear between sections. `reports/s8_cg_limits.json`.
**Trusted as aerodynamics; a stability verdict still needs a mass model.**

**D6 · AVL plus a correction, on a wing it has not seen (next step 14).** Leave one geometry
out, ten times, at `gci_C`. Uncorrected AVL misses CFD CL by rms 0.0216; adding the
per-incidence mean offset learned on the other nine cuts that to **rms 0.0038** (max 0.0094);
CMy to rms 0.0019; the neutral point, with a constant offset, to **2.8 mm rms** (6.2 mm max).
The drag AVL cannot supply — CFD CD minus AVL induced drag — varies only 3.8–4.7 % between
wings at each incidence. `reports/s8_surrogate_check.json`. **The multifidelity plan is
supported, on ten wings at one grid level; drag needs its own model, but a simple one.**

### Physics checks

**E1 · Global mass balance.** Far-field imbalance 6.6e-8 to 1.3e-7 of throughput on both
levels; wall leakage ~1e-9. Density from cp isentropically. **Trusted.**

**E2 · Surface cp above the stagnation bound.** Bound 1.0018 at M 0.0837, recomputed
independently. Peak excess falls 33–72 % with refinement, so it is discretization. The first
verdict read the cell *count*, which rises with refinement, and got the opposite answer; judged
on peak excess. **Trusted.**

### External validation

**F1 · ONERA M6.** *Conditions:* the NASA WIND grid, 294,912 cells, M 0.8395, α 3.06°, Re 11.72e6
on MAC, liftIndex 2, **ANK→NK**. *Reference:* Schmitt & Charpin's measured cp at seven stations.
*Verified:* reference area 0.5263 in semispan units equals the published 0.7532 m² half-area
divided by semispan²; stations read from the data (η = 0.96, not the misprinted 0.95); the
tecplot vertical axis maps to the grid's y. *Result:* suction peak 1.1 %, shock position 0.014
x/c, RMS Δcp 0.15 (0.32 before 15 Sept: every section was cut on the nearest span row and
normalised at the requested station, which slid it along the chord). **Qualitative.** *Update 11 Sept:* ANK-only on the same grid reproduces the NK run exactly
(CL 0.266459, same shock position and suction peak), so the configuration objection is
closed. The coarsened family (4,608 / 36,864 / 294,912 cells) oscillates — CL 0.2458, 0.2686,
0.2665; CD 0.0167, 0.0142, 0.0153 — so it yields no GCI, and the 7 % drag change between the
two finer grids says M6 drag is not grid-converged at 295k. The WIND archive publishes cp
only; there are no forces to compare. Integrated measured cp is the one measured load:
span-integrated normal force **+5.3 %**, stations 2–6 within 4 %, root +16 % (the tunnel-wall
boundary layer is a plausible cause, unverified), tip −15 %. `reports/s8_onera_m6_loads_*.json`.
Still: the headline cp metrics were chosen after the RMS came out poor, and the regime —
transonic, shocked — is not AERIS's.
*Update 15 Sept — the M6 through OUR mesher* (`m6_oh`, 955k cells, converged 9.99e-7): RMS Δcp
0.074 against the NASA grid's 0.15, suction peak 2.6 % against 1.1 %, shock position 0.021
against 0.014 x/c. The shocks are smeared at stations 3–4, mid-chord, where an O-mesh clustered
at both edges is coarsest chordwise, and the tip station's suction peak is 7 % high. Three
comparison bugs were fixed before these numbers: tip-cap cells leaking into every slice, the
off-station slicing above, and the lambda shock's two legs matched crosswise at station 3.
`reports/s8_onera_m6_our_mesh.json`.

**F2 · Transition, bounded (next step 11).** ADflow has no transition model; every S8 run is
turbulent from the leading edge. NeuralFoil (XFOIL-trained) on each wing's own root, mid-span
and tip sections, at their own chord Reynolds numbers and matched lift: fully turbulent section
drag is **1.4–2.0× free-transition drag at root and mid-span** (Re 0.45–1.85 million). At the
tips (Re 1–3e5) it falls below 1: free transition there means laminar separation bubbles,
which forcing transition removes. If the real wing holds laminar flow, S8 overstates its
profile drag by tens of percent — an error no grid refinement can show.
`reports/s8_transition_sensitivity.json`. **A section-model estimate, not a measurement; it
sizes the question.**

**F3 · NACA 0012 low-speed verification (next step 10) — queued.** The TMR case at M 0.15,
Re 6e6, α 10°, whose SA answer three codes have grid-converged: CL 1.0909, CD 0.012273
(CDp 0.006067, CDv 0.006206). pyHyp O-grids of 16k, 65k and 262k cells, because the TMR
repository holds only its two coarsest grids; plus χ 3 freestream turbulence against ADflow's
default (χ ≈ 1.3, which every S8 run used) and AERIS's M 0.0837. First finding, from the
configuration test: **ANK alone stalls on this case** (line-search step 0.01, three variants)
while ANK → NK converges in 56 iterations; a stalled ANK run sat near 3e-6 relative residual
with CD still 1.8 % off. The verification uses ANK → NK, which changes the path, not the
equations.

*Update 13 Sept.* The finest grid (o1024) would not converge with either of two stronger
NK set-ups: both stalled near 2e-4 and hit the time cap. The cause looks like the grid, not the
solver — pyHyp reports a worst cell quality of 0.10 on that level, at the cusped trailing edge
where the O-mesh pinches. Extrapolating from the two grids that did converge, assuming the
scheme's formal second order: **total drag lands within 0.41 % of the TMR**, friction drag
−1.46 %, pressure drag +2.32 %, lift +0.93 %. A gentler build of the third level (half the
cells away from the wall) is queued, as are the two single-change runs that the failure took
with it — AERIS's Mach number and χ 3 — repeated on the 65k grid that converges.

*Update 11 Sept, 10:00.* o256 and o512 meet the campaign rule (o512 stopped on the cycle budget
