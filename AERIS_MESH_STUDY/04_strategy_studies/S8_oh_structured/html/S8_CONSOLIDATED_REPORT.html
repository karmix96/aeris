01what the mesh is

Three blocks, one ring, one H patch

O-H structured: an O-ring around each section, marched outward, stacked spanwise, and closed at the tip by a single H patch. Three volume blocks close the domain. At gci_C that is 603,592 cells, zero folded, wall layer on the loft to machine precision.

The tip closure is the part people get stuck on: the tip ring's 92 nodes cut into four pieces — nose arc 5, upper 43, base 5, lower 43 — and those are exactly the four sides of a 43 × 5 rectangle, because 2(43−1) + 2(5−1) = 92.

on novelty

The topology is not novel. NASA's WINGCAP has offered O-, C- and H-topology tip caps for years, and O-H for a blunt trailing edge is published. What is specific here is a counting constraint (nose arc = base, n_base odd) and verification-first automation. Methods paragraph, not abstract. Full walkthrough: Building the O-H Mesh.

03what it means for the design study

Ranking works today. Absolute drag does not

DSE unblocked

what this licenses

Do: rank designs, quote increments, run the design-space study at gci_C — roughly 1–3 counts of grid scatter against a 5–9 count target. Do not: quote an absolute C_D as converged. Limits: 1–3 counts is a scatter, not a bound (the differences are not monotone), and it rests on three geometries all carrying nearly the same error.

honest reading

Pressure drag moves down on all four — the direction refinement moves it — so the edge scaling does what it was added for. But C_Dv moves up by more than the far field explains, and net C_D changes sign between geometries. Family 2 is a clear improvement on pressure drag and is not unambiguously better on total drag at the coarse level. Also: it was tuned for attached flow. Above stall the held directions are exactly the ones that matter, so family 2 may be worse there.

05incidence range

Usable to 15 degrees

why 15

The gate has two acceptance routes and they disagree up there. All ten pass on residual (6.2–7.8 orders), but the settled-force check fails at α 18 (C_L 9.5e−2 %) and α 20 (1.3e−1 %) against 1.3e−2 % at α 14. The forces start moving before the residual does — unsteadiness seen through a steady solver. C_L_max was never bracketed; it is near 22–24° and was not pursued.

converging is not being correct

Spalart–Allmaras delays stall and over-predicts C_L_max, and there is no experiment here to check against. This scan answers “can it be run”, not “is it right”. Deep post-stall is a different study: URANS or DDES, and a mesh with a finer wake and wall-normal direction — the opposite of family 2.

Consequence for the fine-mesh matrix. F and FF on both families, 2 geometries, through post-stall was 112 solves, ~318 h (nearer 800 with separated cases). Capped at 15° the ladder is −2, 0, 4, 8, 10, 12, 14 — so 56 solves, ~159 h. F only halves it again to 28 solves, ~53 h.

06validation

Nothing has validated the physics yet

open

Two separate questions, and conflating them is what made earlier conclusions useless. Verification — are we solving the equations right? — is what every number above addresses. Validation — are they the right equations? — needs an experiment, and there has not been one.

the pattern

Four of these five are the same failure: a quantity the acceptance metric could not see had drifted. That is PLAN §0.4, and it keeps recurring in new places. The cheapest defence has been to measure what is already on disk before buying new runs — which refuted the wake hypothesis for free and would have caught the C_Dv claim sooner.

08next

Ranked by value per hour

  • Run the production DSE now at gci_C. The cancellation result says increments are usable today; there is no reason to hold the design study hostage to the absolute-drag question.
  • Buy gci_C_chord3 on the cloud (98 chordwise, 1,367,416 cells, built and clean on disk). It needs 13.0 GiB — confirmed by probe, it does not fit here even at 4 ranks. It is the only thing that settles whether p stabilises, which is what blocks a defensible absolute number.
  • Re-scope the cloud batch. gci_F and gci_C_chord2 are chordwise-identical (n_side 75), so gci_F's extra 1.35M cells buy refinement in directions measured not to carry the error. Keep a small global family on one geometry for the formal ASME GCI — a chord-only ladder cannot carry that claim — but do not buy gci_F ten times.
  • F/FF on two geometries, α ≤ 15°: 56 solves, ~159 h; 28 solves and ~53 h if FF is dropped.
  • BlendedNet++ cross-check: 1.9 GB, a handful of cases, half a day. Catches the failure class this project actually has.
  • Start the RIBES data request (grant 632556, Roma Tor Vergata). Long third-party lead time, and it is the only route to validating physics.
