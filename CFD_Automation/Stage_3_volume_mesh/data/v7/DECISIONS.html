The ft2 term changes nothing (0.03 counts), so it is not the 18-count gap to ADflow. 1. The V7 night run, 11–12 Nov

Claude alone, following solve/NIGHT_11NOV.md, with Mike's hard rules (no --open-holdouts, no edits to cfdauto/ or configs/*.yaml, the memory watchdog with every solve, at most two option changes per run).

#WhenWhoDecisionWhy (the evidence)
N111 Nov, eveningClaude One run at a time, never the plan's two lines side by side. The desktop has 6 physical cores and 15.9 GiB, not the 48–52 GB of the plan; one ADflow VVC run peaks at 10–11 GiB.
N211 NovClaude Pass timeLimit=10800.0 instead of the plan's 10800. ADflow refused the int ("Expected data type float"); the run never started. Same value.
N311–12 NovClaude Fix three faults in solve/run_adflow.py (one writer, rank 0; the CGNS history names; ADflow's own lift-distribution writer bug), kept uncommitted as a patch. Every rank wrote the records (a corrupt preflight.json); the force history was silently empty; ADflow 2.13.1 passes "allSurfaces" where it needs the family list. The plan names only the night folder for commits.
N412 Nov, nightClaude Stop A1 (ADflow as written) at 30 min with SIGUSR2, before its cap. Its decay rate fell every 30 iterations (0.0048 → 0.0029 orders per iteration): measured, it could not converge in its cap.
N512 NovClaude ADflow with ANKUseTurbDADI=false (A1b), and A4 (−5°) and A5 (+10°) with it directly. A1's SA residual stalled under the one-sweep DADI turbulence update; with the turbulence by Krylov, 6 orders in 136–239 iterations at three angles. Running A4 and A5 as written would have cost two stalled runs.
N612 NovClaude Launch SU2 with MPICH's mpiexec.hydra, never Open MPI's mpirun. The SU2 8.5.0 binary is a static MPICH build: under Open MPI it ran as separate 1-rank copies (two banners, no partitioning, exit 0). S8's SU2 logs show the same signature.
N712 NovClaude B1's two tries: 30 linear iterations (B1b), then the low-Mach correction (B1c). B1's CFL collapsed (cycling 1–86); the compressible Roe scheme at Mach 0.1 overshot the stagnation pressure (Cp 2.25) and gave a negative pressure drag. The correction removed the overshoot; neither try converged.
N812 Nov, 04:1xClaude B2's second try dropped the 30 linear iterations and kept the low-Mach correction. Try 1 was killed by the watchdog at 11.96 GiB in 32 s; the linear iterations were the part that cost memory (+1.9 GiB on SA).
N911 Nov, 23:53Claude Host-side kill rule raised to the pagefile 2.5 GiB over its start. The first rule (2 GiB) would have killed A1 at its third sample while WSL's swap stayed at 0.001 GiB. It never acted.
N1012 NovClaude Keep the plan's acceptance rule unchanged through the night, and report its flaw. The plan forbade changing it. It needs 225 iterations to judge "settled", so it could not accept ADflow's converged runs (136–239 iterations).
N1112 NovClaude Do not run A2, A3, X1, X2 (VC and light VC). The plan's own memory test: A1's peak scaled to VC is 22 GiB, to light VC 14 GiB, above WSL's 13.65 GiB.

2. The SU2 study, 12–13 Nov

Mike, 12 Nov: "make SU2 work. Find the transition model that works in our cases. Remove the time limiter, just monitor the convergence."

#WhenWhoDecisionWhy (the evidence)
S112 Nov, morningMike No time limit on any run; convergence is watched, not timed. B2b ended at its 150-min cap unconverged with its lift still moving.
S212 Nov, before 08:02Claude Move SU2 to its incompressible solver (INC_RANS, FDS). Mach 0.1 is incompressible; four compressible tries stalled at 2.1–2.8 orders; S8's verified low-speed cases (TMR NACA 0012, flat plates) all ran incompressible. Memory: 7.1 GiB against 9.4 for the compressible SST + LM.
S312 Nov, before 08:02Claude Transition models in this order: SA + LM, SA-BCM, SST + LM. S8's E387 (Re 2 × 105, measured bubble): SA + LM within the measurement on drag with a bubble in the measured region; SST + LM no bubble, 37 counts high, diverging. Our case files use SST + LM.
S412 Nov, 08:10Claude Put the case file's limiter back (R1b), one change. R1, without a limiter, diverged in its start-up at iteration 41–64.
S512 Nov, 08:45Claude A stall rule fixed before it was applied: no new residual low by 0.1 orders in 500 iterations and slower than 1 order per 1,000. Without a time limit, stopping a run had to rest on a rule written in advance, not on judgement after the fact.
S612 Nov, 09:13Mike Stop R1b and continue it at a fixed CFL of 25 (R1c). R1b's forces had settled but its residual crawled (3.2 orders, under 1 order per 1,000) with the CFL pinned at 1; about 3 more hours.
S712 Nov, 10:00Claude Stop R1c under the stall rule and map the residual of every point (D1). Fixed CFL did not help (−0.06 orders per 1,000). D1: 90 % of the pressure residual on about 100 points at two leading-edge corners, the root on the symmetry plane and the planform kink at y/s 0.525.
S812 Nov, 10:12Claude Freeze the limiter after the start-up (LIMITER_ITER). R1d: the residual dropped an order in 40 iterations after the freeze and then fell smoothly; the forces did not move (CD 167.78 → 167.79).
S912 Nov, 10:34Claude Cap the adaptive CFL at 50. R1e: with 10 linear iterations SU2 cannot hold a CFL above about 50; it doubled to 100–220 and was cut by 10 every 4–5 iterations, each cut kicking the residual.
S1012 Nov, 10:37Claude Prove the recipe on SA from the freestream (R1f) before any transition run. R1b–R1e were hand-steered continuations; a recipe has to work alone, and R1f is the one-change baseline of every transition run.
S1112 Nov, 09:20Claude Keep SU2 restart paths under 100 characters. A 101-character restart path made all six ranks abort ("buffer overflow detected"); a short name loaded the same file.
S1213 Nov, 07:09Mike Continue R1f from its last restart; run the rest as a detached queue. WSL shut down at 11:57 on 12 Nov (kernel 6.18.54 → 6.18.58; every process SIGTERM at once; memory fine), killing R1f at 4.69 orders.
S1313 Nov, 07:25Mike New acceptance rule (solve/read_results.py): continuity residual 4 orders down (ADflow 10−4); momentum 2 orders; turbulence and transition not rising; forces flat over the last 20 % (at least 30 iterations): CD 0.5 counts, CL 0.1 %, CMy 0.0005, no drift; oscillating forces never pass. Measured on R1f: CD 9.5 counts from final at 2 orders, 0.46 at 3, 0.07 at 4. ADflow A1 at 4.2 orders: CD within 0.02 counts of its 6-order value. Momentum residuals fall about half as far as continuity. A Fluent/CFX good-practice analogue. Applied to all runs: the converged ADflow runs and R1d–R1f pass; every wrong compressible SU2 run still fails.
S1413 Nov, 07:22Claude The rule's force window has a 30-iteration minimum, not 100. With 100, ADflow's converged runs (137–240 iterations) failed: their window still held the start-up (CD range 1.4–2.2 counts); with 30 it moves 0.002–0.12 counts.
S1513 Nov, 07:27Claude Stop R1f-cont as accepted on its whole record. 4.83 orders, momentum 2.31, CD within 0.035 counts over the last 253 iterations, no drift. SU2 SA: CL 0.19580, CD 167.79 counts, CMy 0.00702.
S1613 Nov, morningMike Test SU2's SA with the ft2 term (R5, SA_OPTIONS= WITHFT2), after R2. SU2 against ADflow at 4°: CL +2.7 %, CD −17.7 counts, almost all friction (104.0 against 117.8). SU2 runs "SA-noft2", ADflow SA with ft2: one change shows whether the SA form is the cause.
S1713 Nov, 09:21Claude Stop SA + LM (R2) under the stall rule; report it as oscillating, mean and band. Residual flat at 3.8–3.9 orders; forces wandering since iteration 600: CL 0.216–0.227, CD 137.4–143.2 counts. Closed laminar bubbles at every station.
S1813 Nov, 09:10Mike Run SA + LM on the light VC mesh (R6) to see whether the swing is numerical. Done: the swing stayed (4.3 against 5.8 counts peak to peak), the bubbles sat in the same places, the drag level fell 10 counts (130.5 against 140.0). Light VC peaked at 11.27 GiB, 1.5 GiB from the watchdog: the largest SA + LM mesh this machine runs.
S1913 Nov, 10:20Claude Record that ft2 is not the SU2–ADflow difference (R5 accepted). SA with ft2: CD 167.76 against 167.79 counts without it; the gap to ADflow is 17.7 counts, 13.8 of them friction. The rest lies in the discretisation, the wall treatment, compressibility and dissipation.
S2013 Nov, 15:10Claude Accept SA-BCM (R3): the only transition model that converges here. 4.93 orders, momentum 2.51, CD within 0.06 counts and CL within 0.09 % over 328 iterations: CL 0.2061, CD 129.95, CMy +0.0065; 7.1 GiB, 2 h.
S2113 Nov, 17:22Claude Stop SST + LM (R4) under the stall rule; oscillating. CD 146.3–152.1 counts, CL 0.229–0.236, the Reθ equation never below −0.65. On the working numerics it closes its bubbles (the night's open separation was the compressible numerics), but they are the longest: most pressure drag.
