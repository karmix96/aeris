Solvers and transition models: what works on the Stage 3 mesh

Correction, 13 Nov 22:20. On the E387 airfoil SU2 SA + LM converged to 6 orders (CL 0.591, CD 112.8 counts against McGhee's 0.574 and 118 ± 2.6; bubble of the measured length), but only after a 3,000-iteration plateau that the stall rule used below would have stopped at 1,425 iterations. The 3-D SA + LM and SST + LM runs were stopped by that rule at 1,423–1,606 iterations, so “they oscillate, only SA-BCM converges” is not established yet. Update, 14 Nov: on E387 SA-BCM equals SA + LM to 0.2 counts (both 5 counts below McGhee). In 3-D, SA + LM continued for 5,700 more iterations ends in a narrow limit cycle, CD 135.3 ± 0.4 counts, CL 0.212 (not 137–143); SA-BCM converges to a steady 130.0 counts, CL 0.206.

  • Both solvers converge on our mesh now, each with one fix. ADflow gives fully turbulent SA only; SU2 adds the transition models.
  • Transition is a first-order effect at this Re: 18 to 38 drag counts below fully turbulent SA (11 to 23 %), almost all of it friction.
  • Of the transition models, only SA-BCM gives a steady, converged answer. The models agree on the flow (laminar bubbles on the upper side) but not on the numbers.

Solvers

ADflow 2.13.1

  • As written the SA residual stalls. With ANKUseTurbDADI=False: 6 orders in 136–239 iterations at −5°, 4°, +10° (31 min at 4°).
  • 4°: CL 0.1907, CD 185.5 counts (pressure 67.6, friction 117.8).
  • No transition model: it can only answer the fully turbulent question.
  • Memory 9.6–10.2 GiB per million cells: the M level needs 70–97 GiB.

SU2 8.5.0

  • The case files' compressible solver never converged (4 tries, 2.1–2.8 orders) and gave a wrong pressure field at Mach 0.1.
  • Fix: incompressible solver, limiter frozen after the start-up, CFL capped at 50. SA converges from the freestream: CL 0.1958, CD 167.8.
  • Cheapest: 6.6 GiB per million cells; 1.6 h on VVC. Has transition models.
  • Against ADflow: CL +2.7 %, CD −17.7 counts (friction −12 %). The SA ft2 term is not the cause (0.03 counts).

All three put laminar separation bubbles on the upper side at x/c 0.34–0.53, reattaching turbulent at 0.4–0.96, and keep the lower side laminar. They differ by 20 counts on CD and in the sign of CMy. The SA + LM swing stayed on the finer mesh, which points to the bubbles themselves; its drag level moved 10 counts with the mesh. There is no measurement on this wing, so no model can be called right from these runs.

Recommendation

  1. ADflow stays the fully turbulent production solver. Put ANKUseTurbDADI: False into case.py and commit the three run_adflow.py fixes.
  2. SU2 becomes the transition solver. Put the working recipe into case.py: incompressible solver, frozen limiter, CFL cap 50.
  3. Use SA-BCM for steady transitional runs, after one check against measurement: S8's E387 airfoil case with McGhee's wind-tunnel data. It's 2-D and takes hours. SA + LM is the model that passed that test before, so keep it as the cross-check and report it as a band. Drop SST + LM.
  4. Treat transitional drag as model- and mesh-dependent to about ±10 counts until the E387 check is done. That is far smaller than the 18–38-count transition effect, but larger than fine design differences.
  5. Accept runs on the adopted rule: residual 4 orders down, momentum 2 orders, turbulence and transition equations not rising, forces flat over the last 20 % of iterations. A run whose forces swing is reported as its mean and band.

Open: the 18-count fully turbulent gap between SU2 and ADflow (it is not the SA form). This machine (16 GB) holds VVC and light VC only; M-level runs need more than 48 GB. Records: data/v7/su2_transition/REPORT.md, data/v7/DECISIONS.html, figure data/v7/su2_transition/walls_all_models.png.
