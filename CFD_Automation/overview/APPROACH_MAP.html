ConclusionThe focus space covers the real wings: most real values sit inside it, and so does the Greek RX-3 family (BWB, AR 8, sweep 33°; LOTUS and Archytas, 8 Nov). The BWB is the extreme shape: a root up to 2.83 mean chords and a tip down to 0.10, up to 17 × smaller. A survey of 202 fixed-wing UAVs (Mitridis et al. 2023) finds the spread inside each NATO class wide (span and cruise speed against weight: R² < 0.2), so a sampled range, not a trend line, is the right description.

Step 3 · Stage 0 → 1

Similarity: size and speed become one number, the Re

At low Mach, the shape, the Re and the angle set the flow. So a small slow wing and a large fast wing of the same shape need the same mesh. We build each shape once, at c̄ = 1, and give it the Re.

First (30 Oct – 6 Nov)
  • each wing at its own size, speed, altitude
  • 21 meshes per wing, about 42,000 solves
  • focus capped at 7 m full span
  • Mach 0.03–0.20 from the real speeds
Now (decided 4–7 Nov, built 7 Nov)
  • each shape at c̄ = 1; Re 1 × 10⁵, 3 × 10⁵, 1 × 10⁶, 4 × 10⁶ as inputs (8 Nov)
  • 4 meshes per wing, about 8,000 solves
  • no size cap: the full span is the aspect ratio
  • Mach 0.1 + Prandtl–Glauert; 0.3–0.5 later (pending)

ConclusionThe four Re levels (since 8 Nov: 1 × 10⁵, 3 × 10⁵, 1 × 10⁶, 4 × 10⁶) cover the real wings (1.22 × 10⁵ – 3.10 × 10⁶) and every cruise point of the Greek tactical UAVs (LOTUS, Archytas, ACTUS; AR 8 assumed); LOTUS 11 m at top speed (up to 6.3 × 10⁶) is an extrapolation. 10⁵ sits 18 % below the slowest real wing, as a margin. Inside one wing the local Re spans far more: 9.9 × 10³ at the smallest BWB tip to 1.1 × 10⁷ at the largest BWB root.

Step 4 · Stage 1 flow study

The flow: long laminar runs and bubbles, not fully turbulent

2-D XFOIL strips along each wing (smooth wing, calm air). At our Re the flow stays laminar for a long way, often forms a laminar separation bubble, and turns turbulent late.

Bubbles by Re

None below 4 × 10⁴ (open separation); long (0.4–0.6 c) at 4 × 10⁴ – 10⁵; almost always (93–99 %) at 1–4 × 10⁵; fading (64 → 26 %) at 5 × 10⁵ – 1.5 × 10⁶; rare above.

Angle

A higher angle moves transition forward: the upper laminar run is 63 % c at 0° and 26 % c at 10°. At −5° the lower side becomes the suction side. Post-stall goes to a separate study.

3-D

Two Re regimes on one BWB (root up to 17 × the tip); crossflow on swept panels (the 2-D strips cannot see it); tip vortex and tip stall; kinks at the breaks.

ConclusionFully turbulent SA puts the profile drag far too high: +59 % (median, 20 reference wings × 4 Re levels, 0–10°, tables rebuilt on the fixed nose; +62 % in the first dataset). So transition must be captured (SU2 SST + LM), and the mesh must be fine where transition moves.

  • band cell = bubble / 20 at C
  • lower band 60–100 % c
  • station gap 0.2 c at C; last tip gap = s0
  • 790 of 790 builds pass (first wings)
Now (decided 4–7 Nov, built 7 Nov)

  • band cell = bubble / 20 at F; 0.5 % c at F from local Re 4 × 10⁵

  • station gap 0.25 c at M; last tip gap = 2 × s0; the nose solved at every level
  • 1,724 of 1,724 builds pass (4 F builds after one nose repair)

ConclusionThe surface mesher did not change, only its rule values. The coarse levels are now coarser (good enough for the SA mesh studies), and F holds the transition targets that SU2 with LM needs.

Step 6 · Stage 3 plan

Volume mesh: one matched C-H mesh for both solvers

SU2 has no overset, so the overset wake box and vortex tube cannot serve both solvers. One C-H mesh can: the C rows wrap the nose, the H lines carry the wake to the far field.

First (30 Oct – 6 Nov)
  • O-H body mesh to about 2 c, background box 0.03 c
  • wake box 0.0075 c to 2 c, then to 10 c; vortex tube 15–20 cells across the core
  • overlap gate (≥ 5 cells); ADflow only
Now (decided 4–7 Nov, built 7 Nov)
  • one matched C-H multiblock mesh; ADflow and SU2 (as unstructured hexes)
  • wake lines to the far field (80 c; ladder 40 / 80 / 160): a rough +30–60 % cells
  • tip vortex resolved in the outboard blocks; no overlap; wall y⁺ ≤ 1 per Re level, growth 1.25 at VC = 1.107 at F

ConclusionThe surface mesh stays the wall of the volume mesh. The cost moves to Stage 3: an open-line march, more wake cells and a tip vortex without its own tube, in exchange for one mesh family and one error bar for both solvers.

ConclusionF is 6.7 M cells (median; largest 10.2 M, at 4 × 10⁶) on the wing blocks, about 9–11 M with the C-H wake lines, inside the target. VC runs on the desktop (0.68 M median); with +36 GB the desktop also takes C and M (wing blocks only); F needs the HPC.
