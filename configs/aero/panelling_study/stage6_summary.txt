4. SELECTION RULE (cheapest passing all 7 by end-to-end time)
   NOTHING PASSES all seven gates. Do not lower a gate. The design tier
   needs a more expensive discretisation than hoped (§7).

4b. GATE-6 DIAGNOSTIC (why the trim gate fails — NOT a gate change)
    Strict gate 6 uses ALL designs. But a few designs need trim deflections
    beyond the +/-25 deg envelope (untrimmable regardless),
    where the deflection magnitude is large and mesh-sensitive. Restricting
    to TRIMMABLE designs isolates the decision-relevant number:
     setting strict maxDdt trimmable maxDdt n untrimmable
       c8s2u      3.406 deg         1.881 deg             7
      c12s2u      1.615 deg         0.950 deg             7
      c16s2u      0.817 deg         0.501 deg             7
      c24s4c      0.692 deg         0.442 deg             7
    -> production (c24s4c) passes gate 6 on trimmable designs (its strict
       failure is one untrimmable outlier); c16s2u sits on the 0.5 deg line.

6. WHAT THIS MEANS
   A cheap mesh is acceptable only if it keeps the design RANKING and the
   trim/stability CLASSIFICATION, not merely the coefficients. The gates
   test that directly. The recommended setting is the cheapest that does.

7. CAVEATS
   - Trimmable label uses a +/-25 deg elevon envelope (documented threshold).
   - Extreme geometries are NOT pooled into these statistics.
   - Comparator (chord48/span2) is itself ~1.5% short on elevon power
     (Stage 2); it is the best available reference, not ground truth.
