Robustness — the confirmation that mattered

The decisive comparisons all ran at the cheap c8s1u mesh, transferred to production by a thin 2-design separability check. That was the biggest weakness, so it was tested directly: re-run the ablation at the production mesh c16s2u (chord-16, span-2), within-family (unbiased).

Bottom line

  1. Placement: settled and robust. Uniform + pinned hard points; adaptive density off. Confirmed at the production mesh; the ablation is within-family and unanimous. This part is safe to adopt.
  2. Count: provisional. N≈25 screening / ~49 tight, on the accuracy axis only — confirm against the trim gate (G4) before locking the number.
  3. Not a complete runbook execution. The DoE-ranking, trim, deflection-transfer, budget-selector and horse-race stages remain. They would not move the placement call, but they are needed to close the study formally and to fully retire the two open weaknesses (#4, #5).
